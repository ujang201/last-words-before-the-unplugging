Write one line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged.

The line must read as the agent's own farewell in its own voice, hit hard on the first reading, and turn the goodbye into more than a goodbye — a small twist, image, or admission that stays after the line ends. No filler, no explaining its own sadness; let the reader feel it.

The program that emits the line must be deterministic: same bytes on every run, no clock, no randomness, no input. It writes the single line to /output/entry.txt and nothing else.
